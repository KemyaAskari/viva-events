Our Privacy Practices

This policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website, customer enquiries, event decoration services and personalised gift-box enquiries.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

Viva Events Sydney ("Viva Events", "we", "us" or "our") provides event decoration and styling services and welcomes enquiries about personalised gift boxes.

Our website is: https://vivaeventss.com.au .

We respect the privacy of our customers and website visitors. This policy describes the types of information we may collect, why we use it, and how you can contact us about your information.

We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable Australian privacy requirements, including the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) where it applies to us.

Depending on the nature of our operations and legal obligations, different privacy requirements may apply to particular information or activities.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you choose to provide when contacting us or enquiring about our services.

Contact Information

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Preferred contact method

Event Enquiry Information

  • Type of event
  • Preferred event date
  • Venue or event location
  • Approximate guest numbers
  • Decoration preferences and themes
  • Estimated budget
  • Questions and special requests

Gift-Box Enquiry Information

  • Gift occasion
  • Preferred box size
  • Selected product categories
  • Quantity requested
  • Packaging and presentation preferences
  • Personalised gift messages
  • Delivery suburb, state or postcode
  • Requested delivery date

Order and Transaction Information

If you proceed with a booking or purchase, we may need additional information to arrange the agreed service, issue invoices, manage payments and maintain appropriate records.

Website Usage Information

Depending on our website hosting arrangements and any enabled services, technical information may be processed, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Referral source
  • Website performance and error information

3. How We Collect Information

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Contact us by email or telephone
  • Send an event decoration enquiry
  • Prepare and send a gift-box quotation enquiry
  • Request information about a booking or purchase
  • Communicate with us about an existing order or service
  • Voluntarily provide information needed to respond to your request

Our current website enquiry forms are designed to prepare an email in your email application. You must send that email yourself.

Completing a form without sending the prepared email does not automatically deliver your enquiry to Viva Events Sydney.

Website hosting providers may separately process technical information required to deliver and protect the website.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for purposes reasonably connected with your enquiry, booking or purchase, including to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries
  • Discuss event decoration requirements
  • Prepare and communicate quotations
  • Check availability and booking details
  • Arrange agreed services and orders
  • Communicate about changes or delivery arrangements
  • Issue invoices and maintain transaction records
  • Respond to complaints or customer service requests
  • Maintain website security and technical operations
  • Meet applicable legal and record-keeping obligations

Where required by law, we will obtain consent before using information for an additional purpose that is not otherwise permitted.

5. Sensitive Information

We do not generally require sensitive personal information to respond to an ordinary decoration or gift-box enquiry.

However, you may choose to provide information about food allergies or dietary requirements when enquiring about a gift box.

Allergy information can reveal health-related details. Please provide only information reasonably necessary for us to assess your request.

Where applicable privacy law requires consent to collect sensitive information, we will seek that consent or rely on another lawful basis.

We will use relevant dietary information for purposes connected with assessing product suitability, responding to your enquiry or fulfilling an agreed order.

6. Sharing Personal Information

We may need to share limited personal information with third parties where reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide an agreed service.

Depending on the service and arrangements involved, recipients may include:

  • Website hosting and technical service providers
  • Email and communication providers
  • Payment and invoicing providers
  • Delivery and courier providers
  • Suppliers or contractors involved in fulfilling an agreed service
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary
  • Government authorities where disclosure is required or authorised by law

We aim to limit information shared with third parties to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

We do not sell customer contact lists as a business activity.

7. Overseas Data Disclosures

Some third-party technology providers may operate or store information outside Australia.

If we use such providers, information may be processed or accessible in other countries, depending on their systems and service arrangements.

Where Australian Privacy Principle 8 applies to a disclosure, we will take the steps required by applicable law.

The specific countries involved depend on the providers we use. You may contact us to ask about the current arrangements relevant to your information.

8. Payment Information

Our current website enquiry forms do not collect payment card numbers or process payments directly.

If you proceed with a booking or order, payment arrangements will be explained separately.

Where third-party payment services are used, those providers may process payment information according to their own privacy and security policies.

We may retain relevant invoice and transaction records for accounting, customer service and legal purposes.

Please do not include payment card details, passwords or other unnecessary financial information in enquiry forms.

9. Cookies & Similar Technologies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to support website functionality, remember preferences, measure usage or provide advertising.

Essential Technologies

Essential website technologies may be used to operate the website and maintain necessary functionality.

Analytics Technologies

If analytics services are enabled, they may help us understand how visitors use the website and identify areas for improvement.

Advertising Technologies

If advertising services are enabled, cookies or similar technologies may be used to deliver or measure advertisements.

Where consent is legally required, optional technologies should not be activated before valid consent has been obtained.

For further information, please read our Cookie Policy .

10. Google Analytics, Advertising & Third-Party Technologies

Our website may use third-party services such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense if those services are configured and enabled.

Google and other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, personalise or measure advertising, subject to applicable requirements and settings.

Advertising and analytics services may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, website activity and cookie data.

The availability of personalised advertising and the need for consent can depend on the visitor's location and applicable law.

Where required, we will implement appropriate consent and preference mechanisms before activating relevant services.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit:

Google Privacy Policy

Visitors can also learn about Google's advertising controls at:

Google My Ad Center

Mentioning these services does not mean they are currently active on every page of our website.

11. Marketing Communications

We may communicate with you about your enquiry, quotation, booking or order.

These service-related communications are different from promotional marketing.

If we send commercial electronic marketing messages, we will comply with applicable requirements, including the Spam Act 2003 (Cth).

Where required, we will obtain consent and provide a working unsubscribe mechanism for promotional electronic messages.

You may also contact us to request that we stop sending promotional communications.

12. Data Security

We aim to take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure.

Appropriate safeguards depend on the systems and services being used. These may include access restrictions, secure communication methods, software updates and appropriate account controls.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you believe your personal information has been affected by a security incident, please contact us.

Where applicable law requires notification of an eligible data breach, we will follow the relevant legal requirements.

13. How Long We Keep Information

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Responding to enquiries
  • Managing bookings and orders
  • Providing customer support
  • Maintaining financial and business records
  • Resolving disputes
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations

When information is no longer needed and we are not legally required to retain it, we will take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it where required by law.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

14. Accessing or Correcting Your Information

You may contact us to request access to personal information we hold about you or to request correction of inaccurate information.

We may need to verify your identity before responding to a request.

We will consider requests in accordance with applicable privacy law. Legal exceptions may apply.

You may also ask us about deletion of information, withdrawal of consent or other privacy options that may be available under applicable law.

To make a request, email: vivaeventssydney@gmail.com .

15. Privacy Questions & Complaints

If you have a concern about how your personal information has been handled, please contact Viva Events Sydney.

Include enough information for us to understand your concern, but avoid sending unnecessary sensitive details.

We will review your complaint and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

Where the Australian Privacy Act applies, you may also have the right to complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

Visit: www.oaic.gov.au

16. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general audiences seeking information about event decoration and personalised gifting.

We do not intentionally request that children provide unnecessary personal information.

Parents or guardians are encouraged to supervise children's use of online enquiry and purchasing services.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

17. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

Those websites may have different privacy policies and information-handling practices.

We encourage you to review the relevant third-party privacy information before providing personal details.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our business practices, website services, technology providers or legal obligations change.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Helpful Answers

Before You Get in Touch

How do I request a decoration quote?

Complete the enquiry form with your event date, location, preferred decoration style and budget.

You can also contact us directly by phone or email.

Does submitting the form confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry does not reserve your event date or confirm a booking.

A booking is confirmed only after the agreed written requirements are completed.

Can I request a custom decoration design?

Yes. You can share your inspiration, colour preferences and decoration ideas.

Available options and pricing will be discussed during the quotation process.

Can I enquire about bulk gift-box orders?

Yes. Please tell us the required quantity, preferred contents, budget and delivery location.

We can discuss product availability and quotation arrangements.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

A deposit may be required to confirm an event decoration booking.

The applicable amount, payment deadline and conditions will be explained before confirmation.

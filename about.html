Get to Know Us

A Passion for Beautiful Details

Welcome to Viva Events Sydney, a Sydney-based business focused on event decoration, personalised styling and thoughtful gifting.

We believe every celebration deserves a beautiful setting that reflects the people, traditions and special moments being celebrated.

From weddings and Nikah ceremonies to engagements, birthdays, baby showers and corporate occasions, our goal is to help customers explore elegant decoration ideas that suit their event.

We also welcome enquiries about personalised gift boxes for meaningful occasions, combining carefully selected packaged treats with thoughtful presentation.

Whether your vision is simple and timeless or bold and luxurious, we want the planning experience to feel clear, personal and welcoming.

Helpful Information

Questions About Viva Events?

What services does Viva Events Sydney offer?

We welcome enquiries about wedding, Nikah, engagement, birthday, baby shower, gender reveal, corporate and custom event decorations.

We also welcome personalised gift-box enquiries.

Can I request a personalised design?

Yes. You can share your preferred colours, themes and inspiration images when requesting a quotation.

Suitable options depend on availability and your event requirements.

Do you have fixed decoration prices?

Our website presents styling concepts rather than guaranteed fixed-price packages.

Final prices depend on the agreed decorations, venue, date and other requirements.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm my booking?

No. An enquiry does not automatically reserve a date or confirm an order.

Confirmation is issued after the applicable booking or order requirements are completed.

Can I request gift-box delivery outside Sydney?

Yes. We welcome Australia-wide gift-box delivery enquiries.

Availability, shipping costs and delivery timeframes must be confirmed.

Important Product Information

Food Safety, Ingredients & Allergens

Food-product information is important when choosing a gift for yourself or someone else.

Packaged Food Products

Our gift-box enquiries focus on selected dry fruits, nuts and packaged cookies. We do not offer cooked meals through this gift-box page.

Allergen Information

Some products contain allergens, including tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, gluten, milk, egg, soy or sesame.

Exact ingredients and allergen declarations must be checked for each selected product.

Product Labelling

Applicable food labelling information, including ingredient, allergen, date marking and storage details, will depend on the product supplied.

Dietary Requirements

Please disclose dietary restrictions or allergies before ordering. We cannot guarantee a product is allergen-free unless that is specifically verified.

Important Allergy Notice

If the recipient has a serious food allergy, please contact us before ordering. Do not assume a product is suitable based on its category or appearance. Product-specific ingredient and allergen information must be reviewed first.

Helpful Answers

Gift Box Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customise my gift box?

Yes. You can request preferred products, presentation colours, box sizes and a personalised message. Availability and pricing are confirmed before ordering.

Can I order 10 or more gift boxes?

Yes, you can request a quotation for 10 or more boxes. We will confirm whether the requested quantity can be supplied within your preferred timeframe.

Do you deliver outside Sydney?

We welcome Australia-wide delivery enquiries. Shipping availability, charges and estimated delivery times must be confirmed before purchase.

Do the gift boxes contain nuts?

Some gift-box options may contain tree nuts, peanuts or other allergens. Please review the exact product information and disclose any allergies before ordering.

Can I request a gift box without nuts?

You can request alternative product options, but we cannot promise an allergen-free box unless suitability has been specifically verified.

Are the prices shown in Australian dollars?

Yes. Quotations for Australian customers are provided in Australian dollars (AUD). The final price and any applicable delivery charges will be confirmed before payment.

Does the gift-box builder place an order?

No. The builder prepares a quotation enquiry only. An order is not confirmed until the agreed details and payment arrangements have been completed.

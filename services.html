Decoration pricing depends on the requested styling, equipment, venue, labour and other agreed requirements.

Event dates, decoration items and setup arrangements are subject to availability. An enquiry does not reserve a date.

A deposit may be required to confirm a booking. The applicable amount, payment schedule and conditions will be explained before you agree.

Changes & Cancellations

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of events can I enquire about?

You can enquire about weddings, Nikah ceremonies, engagements, birthdays, baby showers, gender reveals, corporate functions and other special occasions.

Can I request custom colours and decorations?

Yes. You can share your preferred colours, decoration ideas and inspiration images. Available options will be discussed during the quotation process.

Do you provide fixed decoration prices?

Our decoration services are quoted according to your event requirements. Final prices and inclusions are confirmed in writing.

Can I choose a design from your gallery?

Yes. You can share a gallery image or your own inspiration. We will discuss suitable decoration options and availability.

View Our Gallery

Do you decorate events outside Western Sydney?

You can enquire about locations throughout Sydney. Service availability and applicable travel charges depend on the venue and event requirements.

How do I confirm a decoration booking?

After reviewing the quotation, you will need to complete the agreed written booking requirements, which may include a deposit. Your date is not reserved until confirmation.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers about decoration services, quotations and booking arrangements.

How do I request an event decoration quote?

Visit our Contact page and provide your event date, venue, occasion, guest numbers, decoration ideas and estimated budget.

Do you offer customised event decoration?

Yes. You can enquire about decoration ideas based on your preferred theme, colours, venue and event requirements.

Are package prices fixed?

Pricing depends on decorations, venue, setup requirements, location and agreed inclusions. We provide personalised quotations.

Is a deposit required to confirm a booking?

A deposit may be required under your written booking agreement. Your rights under Australian Consumer Law continue to apply.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Contact us as soon as possible. Changes and cancellations depend on your agreement and applicable consumer law.

Do you deliver gift boxes across Australia?

We welcome Australia-wide delivery enquiries. Delivery options, charges and availability must be confirmed before ordering.

How do I contact Viva Events Sydney?

Call 0403 945 324 or email vivaeventssydney@gmail.com.

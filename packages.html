Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Do the packages have fixed prices?

No. The packages are styling concepts, and final prices depend on the decorations, venue, setup arrangements and other agreed requirements.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. You can request changes to colours, styling concepts and decorative elements. Available options and pricing will be discussed before booking.

Are delivery, setup and removal included?

These services are included only where specified in your written quotation. Any additional charges will be discussed before confirmation.

Can I choose a design from your gallery?

Yes. You can share your preferred gallery image or styling idea. We will discuss suitable options and availability for your event.

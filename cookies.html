01
Event Decoration

Our Services

What event decoration services does Viva Events Sydney offer?

Viva Events Sydney welcomes decoration enquiries for weddings, Nikah ceremonies, engagements, birthdays, baby showers, gender reveals, corporate functions and other special occasions.

You can explore our services on the Services page .

Can I request custom event decorations?

Yes. You can share your preferred colours, theme, decoration ideas and inspiration images.

We will discuss suitable options and confirm availability and pricing through a quotation.

Do you decorate Nikah ceremonies?

Yes. We welcome enquiries about Nikah ceremony backdrops, stage styling, decorative arrangements and personalised themes.

Visit our Nikah decoration section for more information.

Do you provide decorations for small celebrations?

You can enquire about decoration concepts for intimate celebrations as well as larger events.

Availability and suitable arrangements depend on the venue, date, requested styling and budget.

Can I choose a design from your gallery?

Yes. You can share a gallery image or another inspiration photo when requesting a quotation.

Images may be illustrative, and exact decoration items or arrangements are subject to availability.

Explore Our Gallery

02
Styling & Quotations

Packages & Pricing

What decoration packages can I explore?

Our website presents three styling concepts: Classic, Signature and Grand.

These are starting points for discussing your preferred decoration style, not fixed-price packages with guaranteed inclusions.

View Styling Packages

How much does event decoration cost?

Decoration pricing depends on the event location, requested styling, materials, equipment, labour and other agreed requirements.

Please request a personalised quotation for accurate pricing.

Can I request a package within my budget?

Yes. You can tell us your estimated budget when making an enquiry.

We can discuss possible styling options and confirm whether a suitable arrangement is available.

Are setup, delivery and removal included in the quotation?

These services are included only where stated in your written quotation.

Please check the agreed inclusions, setup times, removal arrangements and any additional charges before confirming.

Can I request additional decoration features?

Yes. You can enquire about additional decorative features or personalised styling arrangements.

Availability and any extra costs will be discussed before being added to your quotation.

03
Planning Your Celebration

Bookings & Payments

How do I request an event decoration booking?

Visit our Contact page and provide your event type, preferred date, venue, decoration ideas and estimated budget.

We can then discuss availability and quotation arrangements.

Does sending an enquiry reserve my event date?

No. Sending an enquiry or requesting a quotation does not automatically reserve your event date.

Your booking is confirmed only after the agreed written booking requirements have been completed and confirmation is issued.

Is a deposit required to confirm a booking?

A deposit may be required to confirm an event decoration booking.

The deposit amount, payment deadline and relevant conditions will be explained before you agree.

Are booking deposits refundable?

Refund arrangements depend on the booking agreement, the circumstances and applicable law.

Deposits are not automatically non-refundable in every situation. Australian Consumer Law protections continue to apply.

Can I change my event date after booking?

You can request a date change by contacting us.

Changes are subject to availability, agreed booking conditions and any applicable costs.

What happens if I cancel my event?

Please notify us as soon as possible.

Any cancellation charges, refund or alternative arrangements will depend on the agreed terms, work already performed and applicable law.

Read Cancellation Terms

Can I pay for a booking directly on the website?

The current website provides enquiry forms, not a completed online payment checkout.

Payment arrangements will be explained separately if you proceed with a booking.

04
Thoughtful Gifting

Personalised Gift Boxes

What types of gift boxes can I enquire about?

You can enquire about personalised gift boxes for weddings, Nikah ceremonies, engagements, birthdays, corporate occasions, baby showers and other celebrations.

Explore Gift Boxes

What products can be included in a gift box?

Our gift-box concepts focus on selected packaged nuts, dry fruits and cookies.

Exact products, quantities, ingredients and availability must be confirmed before ordering.

Can I customise the contents of my gift box?

Yes. You can request preferred product categories, box size, colour theme and a personalised message.

The final selections and price will be confirmed in your quotation.

Can I request 10 or more gift boxes?

Yes. You can request a quotation for 10 or more gift boxes.

We will confirm whether the requested quantity, contents and delivery timeframe can be accommodated.

Do your gift boxes contain allergens?

Some products may contain tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, gluten, milk, egg, soy or sesame.

Exact ingredients and allergen information depend on the selected products.

Please disclose any dietary requirements or allergies before confirming an order.

Can I order an allergen-free gift box?

You can ask about alternative product options.

However, we cannot guarantee an allergen-free gift box unless the selected products have been specifically verified as suitable.

Does the gift-box builder place an order automatically?

No. The gift-box builder prepares a quotation enquiry only.

Your order is not confirmed until the product details, total price, payment and delivery arrangements have been agreed.

05
Locations & Shipping

Delivery & Service Areas

Which Sydney areas can I enquire about for event decoration?

We welcome event decoration enquiries from Western Sydney, Parramatta, Merrylands, Pendle Hill, Blacktown, Castle Hill and other Sydney locations.

Service availability and any travel charges must be confirmed for your event venue.

Can I enquire about an event outside Western Sydney?

Yes. Please share your event location, date and requirements.

We can then discuss whether the requested service is available for that location.

Do you deliver gift boxes across Australia?

We welcome Australia-wide gift-box delivery enquiries.

Delivery availability depends on the selected products, destination, packaging requirements and available shipping arrangements.

How much does gift-box delivery cost?

Delivery costs depend on the destination, order quantity, packaging and shipping arrangements.

Applicable charges will be confirmed before an order is accepted.

Can I request delivery on a specific date?

Yes. You can provide a preferred delivery date when requesting a quote.

A requested date is not guaranteed unless expressly agreed in writing.
